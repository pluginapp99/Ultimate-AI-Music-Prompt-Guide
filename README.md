# 🚀 Awesome Suno & Udio Prompt Engineering Guide (2026)

A complete framework for writing better meta-tags, generating consistent song structures, and making your AI-generated music sound like a professional studio release.

## 1. Song Structure Tags (The Blueprint)
AI models need clear structural boundaries. Never just type lyrics. Always wrap your sections in brackets `[]`.
* `[Intro]` - Sets the mood. Try adding instruments here like `[Intro: Acoustic Guitar]`.
* `[Verse 1]` - Keep the narrative moving. 
* `[Pre-Chorus]` - Build the tension.
* `[Chorus]` - The catchy hook. AI will usually increase the energy here.
* `[Bridge]` - Change the tempo or perspective.
* `[Guitar Solo] / [Drop]` - Let the AI show off its instrumental generation.
* `[Outro] / [Fade Out]` - Crucial for avoiding abrupt, glitched endings.

## 2. High-Impact Genre & Style Keywords
Don't just use one genre. Combine descriptors for unique sounds:
* **For Cinematic/Epic:** `Orchestral, Hans Zimmer style, massive brass, sweeping strings, taiko drums, 85 BPM`
* **For Synthwave:** `Retro 80s, analog synth, driving bassline, neon, 120 BPM`
* **For Lo-Fi Chill:** `Dusty vinyl, lo-fi hip hop, jazz chords, relaxed beat, ambient rain`

## 3. Lyric Generation Secrets
* **Syllable Matching:** AI struggles if Verse 1 has 10 syllables per line, and Verse 2 has 25. Keep your rhythm consistent.
* **Phonetic Spelling:** If the AI mispronounces a word, spell it how it sounds (e.g., write "fizz-ick-al" instead of "physical").

## 4. The Final Step: Fixing the "Plastic" Audio (Crucial)
This is where 99% of creators fail. AI audio models often have a severe frequency cutoff around 12kHz to 16kHz, which makes the vocals sound metallic and the drums sound like torn plastic. If you want to upload your track to Spotify or YouTube, the raw export is not enough. 

Instead of buying expensive DAWs (like Ableton) to fix this, the current standard workflow is to use automated web DSPs.
